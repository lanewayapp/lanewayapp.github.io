03 / Evidence, not estimates

Trust Is Built.
One Fact at a Time.

Plausible is not proof. Laneway checks every claim and can return supported, contradicted, or unsupported.

Supported"MiWay route 7 serves stops on Airport Road."MiWay official GTFS schedule feedLists a stop at 6445 Airport Rd.
Contradicted"MiWay route 7 enters Pearson Terminal 1."MiWay official GTFS schedule feedTerminates at City Centre and Westwood Square. Claim dropped.
Unsupported"The rental shuttle leaves from the arrivals level post."Operator website, unreachableShown as unverified, never stated as fact.

Unsourced facts cannot be constructed. Every source stores its URL and fetch time.

06 / Building in the open

Real Progress.
Honest Limits.

Working today

  • Verified multi-modal plans with a source and fetch time per claim
  • Turn-by-turn navigation with off-route detection and Live Activities
  • Live vehicle positions where operators publish realtime feeds
  • Walking routes on real footpaths from OpenStreetMap
  • Saved offline plans for unreliable airport wifi

Not there yet

  • No public release. Nothing to download yet.
  • iOS and Canada only. Coverage grows one feed at a time.
  • No booking or payments for individual legs.
  • Some sources block crawlers. Those legs return unverified.
Feeds supported
  • UP Express
  • GO Transit
  • TTC
  • MiWay
  • Calgary Transit
