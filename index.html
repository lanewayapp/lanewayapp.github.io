Maps route across a graph. Some edges were never in it.

Mapping apps are excellent at what they have data for. They route across digitized road and transit graphs, and anything missing from that graph is invisible to them. Private shuttles, terminal interiors, courtesy vans, informal connections: the edges do not exist, so the answer that comes back is technically valid and practically useless.

Toronto Pearson Terminal 1 to an off-airport rental depot is the case Laneway is tested against, and the one you just travelled.

Never present a fact the system cannot back up.

Plausible and correct look identical on a page, which is why nobody trusts a generated itinerary. Every claim is checked against a retrieved source before you see it, and the checker is allowed to say no. Three verdicts, never collapsed into two. These are real results from the engine.

MiWay official GTFS schedule feedThe feed lists a stop at 6445 Airport Rd.

Contradicted

"MiWay route 7 enters Pearson Terminal 1."

MiWay official GTFS schedule feedRoute termini are City Centre and Westwood Square. The claim was dropped.

Unsupported

"The rental shuttle leaves from the arrivals level post."

Operator website, unreachableShown as unverified, never quietly deleted and never stated as fact.

A fact without provenance is a bug, so the type system refuses to build one: inside the engine an unsourced fact is unconstructible. Every source carries the URL it came from and the moment it was fetched.

A slow layer that thinks, a fast layer that checks.

The model proposes and never gets the last word. Retrieval, caching and composition are deterministic with no model calls inside them, which keeps both the cost and the failure modes predictable.

Working today

  • Verified multi-modal plans with a source and a fetch time on every claim
  • Turn-by-turn navigation with off-route detection and a lock screen Live Activity
  • Live vehicle positions where the operator publishes a realtime feed
  • Walking routes on real footpaths from OpenStreetMap, not around the block
  • Saved plans that open offline, because airport wifi fails exactly when you need the plan

Not there yet

  • No public release. There is nothing to download, and no App Store listing.
  • iOS only, and Canada only, on purpose. Coverage grows one transit feed at a time.
  • No voice guidance, and no booking or payment for any individual leg.
  • Some sources block crawlers. When that happens the leg comes back honestly unverified.
